护甲减伤

护甲减伤的公式与实测

代码推导

CombatRules.getDamageAfterAbsorb（CombatRules.java:19）：

toughness = 2 + armorToughness / 4 realArmor = clamp(armor − damage / toughness, armor × 0.2, 20) damage' = damage × (1 − realArmor / 25) 护甲 20 / 伤害 9： realArmor = clamp(20 − 4.5, 4, 20) = 15.5 damage' = 9 × (1 − 15.5 / 25) = 3.42 最终生命 = 20 − 3.42 = 16.58
